King Crimson Requiem (KCR)

King Crimson Requiem (KCR)—often localized as Emperor Crimson Requiem or formerly known as Scarlet King Requiem—is one of the most iconic and formidable Stands in the Roblox fighting game Your Bizarre Adventure (YBA). Representing a hypothetical scenario where Diavolo successfully claimed the Requiem Arrow instead of Giorno Giovanna, KCR combines overwhelming raw damage, reality-bending mobility, and oppressive crowd control.
